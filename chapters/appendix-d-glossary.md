# Appendix D: Glossary

**Arc**
: A directed connection between a place and a transition (or vice versa), defining the flow relation.

**Firing**
: The act of a transition consuming tokens from its input places and producing tokens in its output places.

**Incidence matrix**
: A matrix encoding the net effect of each transition on each place.

**Inhibitor arc**
: An arc that prevents a transition from firing when the source place has tokens.

**Marking**
: The distribution of tokens across all places; the state of the net at a given moment.

**Mass-action kinetics**
: A rate law where transition firing rate is proportional to the product of input place token counts.

**P-invariant**
: A weighted sum of place markings that remains constant regardless of which transitions fire.

**Petri net**
: A bipartite directed graph with places, transitions, and arcs, plus a token-based execution semantics.

**Place**
: A node representing a condition or resource; holds tokens.

**Reachability**
: The set of all markings obtainable from an initial marking through sequences of transition firings.

**Token**
: A marker in a place representing the presence of a resource or the truth of a condition.

**Transition**
: A node representing an event or action; fires by consuming and producing tokens.
